## Context

AquaOptim is an innovation project developed for EFREI's Poster Prize 2026. 
Built with a project budget of only €150, it was awarded 2nd place in the 
competition and received a €400 cash prize.

The project addresses a concrete issue: the early detection of water
micro-leaks in commercial buildings. These leaks can remain unnoticed for long
periods of time and are difficult to detect using traditional water meters,
especially when they involve very low and continuous flow rates.

AquaOptim proposes a **non-invasive solution** based on the analysis of
vibrations generated on the surface of water pipes. The objective is to combine
IoT sensors, signal processing, and an anomaly detection model to automatically
identify abnormal behavior that may indicate a leak, without requiring any
drilling, pipe modification, or water supply interruption.

The project therefore covers the entire monitoring pipeline, from vibration
acquisition and signal analysis to anomaly detection and visualization through
a dedicated monitoring dashboard.
